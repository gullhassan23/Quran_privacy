Privacy Policy

Al Quran – Prayer Times & Azan · Pixora Studio

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Al Quran – Prayer Times & Azan application, published by Pixora Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the app.

In short: The app does not require an account. We do not sell your personal information or use advertising or analytics tracking tools. Information you enter or save in the app is generally stored on your device. Third-party services may process technical information when your device connects to them to retrieve online content.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or messages through our own servers.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tracking tools.
  • Third-party content services may process standard technical information when your device connects to them.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Certain information may be stored locally on your device to provide the app's features.

Profile Information

  • An optional name you enter may be used to personalize the app experience.
  • An optional profile picture you select may be stored within the app's private storage.

Location

  • If you grant permission, your device location may be used to calculate prayer times for your area.
  • Location may be processed locally by the app. Third-party location services, if used, may have their own data practices.
  • If location permission is not granted, the app may use an approximate location where supported.

App Preferences and Content

  • Language, appearance, text size, translation choices, prayer calculation settings, and alarm preferences.
  • Reading progress, last-read position, bookmarks, and tasbih count.
  • Downloaded Quran translations and other content used for faster loading or offline access.

We do not intentionally transmit locally stored information to our own servers. Online services may independently process technical information as described in this policy.

3. Permissions

The app may request permissions necessary to provide its features, including:

Permission Purpose
Location To calculate prayer times for your area, if permitted.
Notifications To provide prayer-time reminders and notifications.
Alarms and reminders To schedule prayer-time alarms where supported.
Audio playback To play Azan and other supported audio content.
Vibration To provide alarm and tasbih vibration feedback.
Background operation To support scheduled reminders and audio playback.
Internet access To retrieve online Quran translations, Hadith, fonts, and audio content.

Available permissions can be managed through your device settings. Disabling certain permissions may affect related features.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services to provide online content and certain features. When your device connects to these services, they may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, request time, and connection information.

The services used may include:

  • QuranEnc: Quran translations.
  • AlQuran Cloud: Quran-related content and information.
  • QuranicAudio: Audio recitations.
  • Hadith API and jsDelivr CDN: Hadith text and translations.
  • Google Fonts: Fonts used to display app content.
  • Google Play services: In-app review functionality.
  • Device location services: Location information when permission is granted.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage users to review the relevant policies for more information about how their information is handled.

Quran Arabic text and certain reading resources may be available within the app without an internet connection.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share personal information collected by us for advertising purposes. Third-party providers may process technical information when delivering content or functionality to the app.

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Locally stored information remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app.
  • You can remove optional profile information through the relevant app settings.
  • You can clear locally stored app data through your device settings or uninstall the app.
  • We do not maintain user accounts or a personal-data deletion system on our own servers.

7. Security

We aim to protect information handled by the app through appropriate practices. Information stored locally is subject to your device's security settings. Online connections use HTTPS where supported by the relevant service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and families. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our own servers.

If you have concerns about children's privacy in connection with the app, please contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or our practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
