Privacy Policy

Al Quran – Prayer Times & Azan · by Pixora Studio

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Al Quran app (package name com.pixorastudio.quranpak), published by Pixora Studio ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app on Android (and iOS, where available).

In short: the app does not require an account, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter or choose in the app (name, photo, location for prayer times, settings, bookmarks) is stored only on your device.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or messages.
  • We do not use advertising, ad identifiers or analytics/tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information about you.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone, is used only to make the app work, and is never sent to us:

Profile (optional)

  • Name: if you type a name in Settings, it is shown in the greeting on the Home screen.
  • Profile photo: if you choose a picture, the app uses the Android photo picker to get only that one picture and saves a copy inside the app's private storage. The app does not access your other photos.

Location (optional)

  • With your permission, the app reads your device location to calculate prayer times for where you are. The calculation happens on your device.
  • The saved coordinates stay on your device and are not sent to us or to any server.
  • If you do not allow location, the app uses an approximate city based on your phone's time zone.

App data

  • Settings (language, dark/light mode, text size, translation choice, prayer calculation method, alarm tone and alarm times).
  • Reading progress, last read surah, bookmarks and tasbih count.
  • A copy of downloaded Quran translations and lists so the app opens faster and works offline.

3. Permissions and why we use them

PermissionWhy the app uses it
LocationTo calculate accurate prayer times for your place (optional).
NotificationsTo show prayer-time alarms and reminders.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarm)So the Azan rings exactly at prayer time.
Full-screen notificationTo show the alarm screen on the lock screen when the Azan rings.
Foreground service (media playback)To play the Azan / alarm sound until you stop it.
VibrationAlarm vibration and the gentle vibration of the tasbih counter.
Wake lockTo keep the screen on while the alarm rings.
Run at startupTo set your prayer alarms again after the phone restarts.
InternetTo load translations, Hadith and audio recitations.

You can turn off any permission at any time in your phone's Settings. Some features (for example alarms or exact prayer times) may then not work fully.

4. Content services used by the app

To show content, the app downloads it from the services below. When your device connects to them, they may receive standard technical information that every internet request contains (such as your IP address, device/browser type and the time of the request). We do not send them your name, photo, location or any other personal data. Their own privacy policies apply.

  • QuranEnc.com: Quran translations
  • alquran.cloud: Surah list, Sajdah list, Ayah of the Day, Arabic Tafsir
  • quranicaudio.com: Qari list and audio recitations
  • hadithapi.com and jsDelivr CDN (fawazahmed0/hadith-api): Hadith text and translations
  • Google Fonts: fonts used to display the app's text
  • Google Play In-App Review: shows Google's rating window once; the review is handled by Google
  • Android location services: provide your device location when you allow it

The Quran Arabic text and the Mushaf font (King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex) are included inside the app and work without internet.

5. Data sharing

We do not share any personal information with third parties, because we do not collect it. We may disclose information only if required by law, and we have no personal information about our users to disclose.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • All data described in section 2 stays on your device until you change or remove it.
  • You can remove your name or photo in Settings → Profile.
  • You can delete all app data at any time with Phone Settings → Apps → Al Quran → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Because we do not keep any data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Content is downloaded over secure HTTPS connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we do our best to protect the app and its users.

8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and families. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Please check this page from time to time.
